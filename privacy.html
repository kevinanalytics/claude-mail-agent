Privacy Policy

Claude Mail Agent · Last updated 5 October 2026

Who uses this app

Claude Mail Agent is a personal tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google scope gmail.modify), the app reads email messages, labels and attachments in the owner's Gmail account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's email-processing rules: reading and classifying incoming mail, changing labels and read/unread status, and inserting notes into the owner's own mailbox. The app does not send, forward or delete email.

Where the data goes

The app runs on the owner's own computer. The Google sign-in token is stored only on that computer. To apply the rules, email content is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI on the owner's behalf. No data is sold, shared with other parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
