Claude Mail Agent

A personal email tool. Not offered to the public.

Claude Mail Agent is a private tool used by its owner to organise their own Gmail inbox. It reads incoming mail in the owner's account, applies the owner's processing rules (for example labels and read/unread status), and adds short notes into the owner's own mailbox.

It never sends, forwards or deletes email, and it is used only with the owner's own Google account.
